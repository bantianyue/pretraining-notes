Abstract摘要

Matrix-orthogonalization-based optimizers, exemplified by Muon, have demonstrated strong convergence behavior across a wide range of modern deep learning workloads. The matrix-aware updates offer a compelling alternative to conventional element-wise optimization, particularly as model architectures continue to grow in scale and heterogeneity. Yet contemporary distributed training infrastructure built around the assumption of element-wise optimizers is poorly matched to matrix-level optimizers such as Muon, whose updates couple entire weight matrices and require costly Newton-Schulz iterations. Vanilla Muon implementations incur more than 2× the cost of forward and backward passes. To close this gap, we present DMuon, an open-source distributed Muon implementation that integrates into existing training pipelines as a drop-in module, with no framework-level modifications. Across both embodied foundation model and large language model (LLM) training workloads, DMuon achieves a 1.48×–3.01× speedup in end-to-end step time and a 6.85×–163.00× speedup in optimizer-step time, bringing per-step latency to near-AdamW levels and enabling efficient scaling in our model training.

以 Muon 为代表的、基于矩阵正交化的优化器，已在广泛的现代深度学习负载上展现出很强的收敛表现。随着模型架构在规模与异构性上持续增长，这类能感知矩阵结构的更新为传统的逐元素优化提供了一个有吸引力的替代方案。然而，当代分布式训练基础设施是围绕「逐元素优化器」这一假设搭建的，与 Muon 这类矩阵级优化器并不匹配——后者的更新会耦合整个权重矩阵，并且需要开销高昂的 Newton–Schulz 迭代。朴素实现的 Muon，其开销超过前向与反向传播之和的 2×。为弥合这一差距，我们提出 DMuon：一个开源的分布式 Muon 实现，可作为即插即用模块接入现有训练流水线，无需任何框架层改动。在具身基础模型与大语言模型（LLM）两类训练负载上，DMuon 在端到端单步时间上取得 1.48×–3.01× 的加速，在优化器单步时间上取得 6.85×–163.00× 的加速，把每步延迟拉低到接近 AdamW 的水平，从而使我们能够高效地扩展模型训练。

Large-model training has begun to move beyond the element-wise optimization paradigm that has dominated since AdamW (16). Matrix-aware optimizers, most prominently Muon (11), apply a Newton–Schulz iteration to the momentum-aggregated gradient of each weight matrix, producing updates whose singular values are driven toward a near-uniform spectrum. Recent works suggest that this update geometry translates into meaningful practical benefits, Moonlight (15) reports approximately 2× higher compute efficiency than AdamW under compute-optimal pretraining, and Kimi-K2, DeepSeek-V4 (17, 7) have adopted Muon in production-scale training, providing further evidence that matrix-aware optimization is viable at frontier-model scale. The algorithmic promise of matrix-aware optimizers is therefore increasingly well established. Their deployment cost, however, remains a significant practical obstacle.

大模型训练已开始走出自 AdamW (16) 以来长期占据主导的逐元素优化范式。矩阵感知（matrix-aware）优化器中最具代表性的是 Muon (11)：它对每个权重矩阵的动量累积梯度施加 Newton–Schulz 迭代，使更新结果的奇异值谱趋于接近均匀。近期工作表明，这种更新几何能转化为实实在在的收益——Moonlight (15) 报告称，在计算最优预训练下其计算效率比 AdamW 高约 2×；Kimi-K2、DeepSeek-V4 (17, 7) 已在生产规模训练中采用 Muon，进一步说明矩阵感知优化在前沿模型规模上是可行的。因此，矩阵感知优化器在算法层面的前景已日益明确。但它们的部署成本，仍然是显著的实际障碍。

Modern distributed training stacks execute the optimizer step on shards of each parameter, one shard per rank. The implicit contract is that the optimizer’s update rule is element-wise: AdamW’s updates apply to a shard with no knowledge of any other shard. Muon breaks this contract. Its core computation, the Newton–Schulz iteration, operates on the full weight matrix rather than individual shards. Before a Muon update can be computed, the matrix must therefore be reconstructed, introducing communication that is absent from conventional element-wise optimizers. This additional communication is not a minor overhead. It appears at every optimizer step, for every matrix parameter, and scales with model size and distributed width. In practice, the cost of distributed Muon can rival or even exceed the combined wall-clock time of the forward and backward passes.

现代分布式训练栈在执行优化器步骤时，是对每个参数的分片分别计算，一个 rank 负责一个分片。这里隐含的契约是：优化器的更新规则是逐元素的——AdamW 的更新作用在一个分片上时，不需要知道任何其他分片。Muon 打破了这个契约。它的核心计算 Newton–Schulz 迭代，作用在完整的权重矩阵上，而不是单个分片。因此，在能够计算 Muon 更新之前，必须先重建矩阵，这引入了传统逐元素优化器中不存在的通信。这部分额外通信并非微不足道的小开销：它在每个优化器步骤、对每个矩阵参数都会出现，并且随模型规模与分布式宽度增长。实践中，分布式 Muon 的开销可以与前后向传播的总墙钟时间相当，甚至更高。

The extra per-step cost erodes Muon’s FLOP-level advantage and weakens the wall-clock incentive to deploy it at scale. The penalty is particularly pronounced for embodied-model training. Compared to contemporary LLM and VLM pretraining (18, 23, 24, 3), whose forward–backward passes typically involve substantially more computation per optimizer step, VLA training (4, 10, 12, 33, 32, 31) typically uses much shorter temporal contexts. As a result, the forward–backward phase occupies a much smaller fraction of the step time, making optimizer overhead significantly harder to amortize.

2.2Sharded Training Abstractions分片训练的抽象

Distributed training systems partition weight matrices in different ways. The location of shard boundaries determines how a matrix is stored, communicated, and reconstructed during training. We briefly review the three sharding schemes most relevant to matrix-aware optimization: ZeRO-style sharding, FSDP-style sharding, and tensor parallelism.

分布式训练系统以不同方式划分权重矩阵。分片边界落在哪里，决定了矩阵在训练过程中如何存储、如何通信、如何重建。我们简要回顾三种与矩阵感知优化最相关的分片方案：ZeRO 风格分片、FSDP 风格分片，以及张量并行。

ZeRO-style: flatten then shard.ZeRO 风格：先拍平，再分片。

ZeRO (19) (as realized by DeepSpeed (20)) first flattens a group of parameters into a single contiguous buffer and then partitions that buffer across data-parallel ranks. Shard boundaries are defined by storage layout rather than tensor structure, so a single matrix may span multiple ranks and share a shard with unrelated parameters.

ZeRO (19)（由 DeepSpeed (20) 实现）先把一组参数拍平进一个连续缓冲区，再把这个缓冲区划分到各个数据并行 rank 上。分片边界由存储布局而非张量结构决定，因此单个矩阵可能横跨多个 rank，并与无关的参数共享同一个分片。

FSDP: per-tensor sharding.FSDP：按 tensor 分片。

FSDP2 (35) represents each parameter as a DTensor sharded along its leading dimension. Unlike ZeRO-style sharding, partition boundaries align with tensor structure, making reconstruction and communication local to an individual parameter. HSDP extends the same abstraction to a two-dimensional Dshard× Dreplica mesh to reduce communication distance in multi-node deployments.

FSDP2 (35) 把每个参数表示为一个沿首维分片的 DTensor。与 ZeRO 风格分片不同，这里的划分边界与张量结构对齐，使重建与通信都局限在单个参数之内。HSDP 把同一套抽象扩展到二维的 Dshard× Dreplica 网格，以缩短多机部署中的通信距离。

Tensor parallelism: shard along a semantic axis.张量并行：沿语义轴分片。

Tensor parallelism (TP) (22) partitions weight matrices along model-semantic dimensions such as attention heads or MLP hidden channels. These partitions persist throughout execution and therefore become part of the model definition itself rather than merely a storage strategy.

张量并行（TP）(22) 沿模型语义维度划分权重矩阵，例如注意力头或 MLP 隐层通道。这些划分在整个执行过程中持续存在，因此成为模型定义本身的一部分，而不只是一种存储策略。

2.3Problems and Opportunities问题与机会

Granularity mismatch. Given the sharding schemes in §2.2, the conflict with Muon is immediate: training systems expose matrix parameters in pieces, while Muon must run Newton–Schulz on a full reduced matrix gradient. As illustrated in Figure 2, for a matrix parameter W∈ℝm× n, Muon requires M=1D∑r=1Dgr before applying Eq. 2. Currently, a distributed system must therefore materialize the full matrix somewhere before running the Newton–Schulz iteration.

粒度错配。给定 §2.2 中的分片方案，与 Muon 的冲突立刻显现：训练系统暴露出来的是被切成碎片的矩阵参数，而 Muon 必须在一个完整的、已规约的矩阵梯度上运行 Newton–Schulz。如图 2 所示，对于矩阵参数 W∈ℝm× n，Muon 在施加式 2 之前需要 M=1D∑r=1Dgr。因此，现有的分布式系统必须在某处先把完整矩阵物化出来，才能运行 Newton–Schulz 迭代。

A straightforward way to satisfy this full-matrix requirement is gather-then-compute. Each rank materializes the full reduced matrix gradient, runs the Newton–Schulz iteration locally, and then keeps the portion of the resulting update corresponding to its local parameter shard. This strategy is simple and preserves Muon’s update rule, but it exposes the two costs that an efficient distributed system must address: matrix materialization and redundant orthogonalization.

满足这一「完整矩阵」要求的一种直接做法是「先聚合、再计算」（gather-then-compute）。每个 rank 物化出完整的规约矩阵梯度，在本地跑一遍 Newton–Schulz 迭代，然后只保留与本地参数分片相对应的那部分更新。这种做法简单，并且保持了 Muon 的更新规则，但它暴露了高效分布式系统必须解决的两项开销：矩阵物化，以及重复正交化。

Matrix materialization. At every optimizer step, the full reduced matrix must be reconstructed from its distributed representation. This introduces optimizer-specific collective communication whose volume scales with matrix size and distributed width. The cost is especially pronounced because Muon operates at the level of individual matrices: each matrix must be made available as a coherent object before the Newton–Schulz iteration can begin.

矩阵物化。每个优化器步骤都必须从分布式表示中重建完整的规约矩阵。这引入了优化器特有的集合通信，其通信量随矩阵规模与分布式宽度增长。代价之所以格外突出，是因为 Muon 是在单个矩阵的层面上运作的：每个矩阵都必须先成为一个完整、一致的对象，Newton–Schulz 迭代才能开始。

Replicated orthogonalization. Once every rank has materialized the same matrix, the Newton–Schulz iteration is executed identically on all ranks. Aggregated optimizer compute is therefore multiplied by the number of ranks that redundantly run the same orthogonalization, even though each matrix only needs to be orthogonalized once. Since this iteration is already Muon’s primary computational bottleneck, such redundant execution can cause the optimizer step to rival or exceed the combined cost of the forward and backward passes at scale.

重复正交化。一旦每个 rank 都物化出同一个矩阵，Newton–Schulz 迭代就会在所有 rank 上被一模一样地各执行一遍。于是聚合后的优化器计算量，被「重复执行同一正交化的 rank 数」放大——尽管每个矩阵只需要被正交化一次。由于这一迭代本就是 Muon 的主要计算瓶颈，在大规模下这种重复执行会让优化器步骤的开销与前后向传播之和相当，甚至更高。

Intuitively, the replication problem can be alleviated through owner-like execution, where each matrix is assigned to a designated owner. Yet removing redundant computation alone is not enough: communication and execution overheads quickly become the new bottlenecks. DMuon addresses these through a multi-level co-designed architecture, forming an integrated solution for efficient distributed Muon training (§3).

直观上，重复计算问题可以通过类似「owner」的执行方式来缓解：把每个矩阵指派给一个指定的 owner。但仅仅去掉重复计算还不够：通信与执行开销很快会成为新的瓶颈。DMuon 通过一套多层次协同设计的架构来解决这些问题，形成一个面向高效分布式 Muon 训练的整体方案（§3）。

3System Design系统设计

Our goal is to make Muon practical in sharded distributed training while preserving its exact update rule, and simultaneously reduce its per-step overhead to a near-AdamW level. We address this challenge by employing system-level design rather than isolated optimizations: DMuon orchestrates runtime, scheduling, and kernel-level techniques to mitigate the granularity mismatch identified in §2.3. In this section, we present the overall system design of DMuon.

我们的目标是让 Muon 在分片分布式训练中真正可用：既保持其更新规则完全不变，又把单步开销降到接近 AdamW 的水平。我们不是做零散的局部优化，而是用系统级设计来应对这一挑战——DMuon 在运行时、调度与 kernel 三个层面协同编排，以缓解 §2.3 指出的粒度错配。本节介绍 DMuon 的整体系统设计。

3.1Design Overview设计概览

Figure 3 contrasts the gather-then-compute baseline with DMuon’s owner-style execution. In the baseline, each rank materializes the full gradient of every matrix parameter and runs the same Newton–Schulz iteration locally, so optimizer compute is replicated across the data-parallel group. DMuon instead assigns each matrix parameter to a single owner rank. The owner maintains the authoritative optimizer state for that matrix and computes its Muon update once, while non-owner ranks materialize the parameter only when it is needed by forward or backward computation.

图 3 对比了「先聚合再计算」基线与 DMuon 的 owner 式执行。在基线中，每个 rank 都物化每个矩阵参数的完整梯度，并在本地跑同一套 Newton–Schulz 迭代，因此优化器计算在整个数据并行组内被复制了一份份。DMuon 则把每个矩阵参数指派给唯一的 owner rank：owner 维护该矩阵的权威优化器状态，并且只计算一次它的 Muon 更新；非 owner rank 只在前后向计算需要用到该参数时才去物化它。

This change removes redundancy, but it also turns distributed Muon into an owner-side execution problem: gradients must be routed to owners, updated parameters must be made visible to non-owners, owner workloads must be balanced, and the remaining Newton–Schulz computation must run efficiently. DMuon addresses these requirements through a coordinated system design rather than a single kernel or communication primitive.

这一改动消除了冗余，但也把分布式 Muon 变成了一道「owner 侧执行」的工程题：梯度必须被路由到 owner；更新后的参数必须让非 owner 也能看到；各个 owner 的负载必须均衡；剩下的 Newton–Schulz 计算必须跑得高效。DMuon 靠一套协同的系统设计来满足这些要求，而不是靠单个 kernel 或某一种通信原语。

At a high level, one DMuon training step proceeds in four phases:

总体而言，一次 DMuon 训练步骤分为四个阶段：

  1. Parameter materialization. Before a layer executes, the matrix parameters required by that layer are materialized from their owners into packed buffers consumed by the forward computation. This makes owner-held parameters appear to the model as ordinary local tensors during execution.参数物化。某一层执行之前，该层所需的矩阵参数会从各自的 owner 物化进打包缓冲区（packed buffer），供前向计算消费。这样在模型看来，owner 持有的参数在执行期间与普通的本地张量没有区别。
  2. Gradient routing. During backward, gradients are accumulated into the same packed buffers and then reduced to the corresponding owners. The reduction produces the same averaged full-matrix gradient that a synchronous Muon reference would use, but materializes it only at the owner.梯度路由。反向过程中，梯度被累积进同样的打包缓冲区，然后再规约到对应的 owner。规约产生的是与同步 Muon 参考实现相同的、取平均后的完整矩阵梯度，但只在 owner 这一处物化。
  3. Owner-side Muon update. Each owner runs the Muon update for its assigned matrices and applies the result to its local authoritative copy. 3.2Owner Centric Communication Optimization以 owner 为中心的通信优化

Under the owner-style execution, each matrix update is computed once by an owner rank, and the updated parameter must be made available to the ranks that execute subsequent forward and backward computation. This removes replicated Newton–Schulz computation, but also gives owner communication a distinct asymmetric structure. In the forward pass, updated parameters are published from one owner to many consumers. In the backward pass, gradients from many ranks are reduced back to the owner. This asymmetry creates an optimization opportunity beyond simply replacing one collective with another.

在 owner 式执行下，每个矩阵更新由某个 owner rank 计算一次，而更新后的参数必须提供给执行后续前向、反向计算的那些 rank 使用。这消除了重复的 Newton–Schulz 计算，但也让 owner 通信呈现出明显的不对称结构：前向阶段，更新后的参数由单个 owner 发布给多个消费者；反向阶段，来自多个 rank 的梯度被规约回该 owner。这种不对称带来了一个机会——不只是用一种集合通信去替换另一种。

We exploit this opportunity with a communication runtime tailored to the owner-style access pattern. Rather than treating parameter publication and gradient routing as uniform all-rank collectives, the runtime organizes them around the hierarchy of the training mesh and schedules them to expose overlap with computation. The remainder of this section shows how this communication structure is mapped onto the training mesh and pipelined across the forward and backward passes to reduce effective communication overhead.

我们用一个专门适配 owner 式访问模式的通信运行时来把握这一机会。该运行时不再把参数发布与梯度路由当作「所有 rank 一视同仁」的集合通信，而是围绕训练网格的层级结构来组织它们，并给它们安排调度以与计算重叠。本节余下部分说明这套通信结构如何映射到训练网格上、如何跨前向与反向做成流水，从而降低有效通信开销。

Beyond occupancy recovery through batching, the Gram-space formulation exposes a second optimization opportunity through symmetry. The Gram matrix G=XX⊤ is symmetric by construction, so computing all m2 entries with a general GEMM performs substantial redundant arithmetic. We therefore employ a SYRK-style execution path that computes only the lower triangular portion of G in the mainloop and reconstructs the upper triangle in the epilogue. This preserves the dense output required by subsequent Gram NS steps while nearly halving the arithmetic work of the dominant Gram update. The symmetry-aware implementation is shared by both the batched and non-batched execution paths. Furthermore, the elementwise operations immediately following the Gram update are fused into the same epilogue, allowing intermediate values to be consumed before they are written back and reloaded from global memory. This eliminates an additional kernel invocation and avoids a separate memory round-trip for the elementwise stage.

除了靠批量化恢复占用率，Gram 空间的形式还借对称性带来第二个优化机会。Gram 矩阵 G=XX⊤ 在构造上就是对称的，因此用通用 GEMM 计算全部 m2 个元素会做大量冗余算术。我们因此采用 SYRK 风格（symmetric rank-k update）的执行路径：主循环中只计算 G 的下三角部分，在上三角于尾声（epilogue）中重建。这样既保留了后续 Gram NS 步骤所需的稠密输出，又把主导的 Gram 更新算术量近乎减半。这套对称性感知的实现被批量化与非批量化两条执行路径共用。此外，紧跟 Gram 更新之后的逐元素操作被融合进同一个尾声，使中间结果在被写回、再从全局内存重新加载之前就被消费掉。这省掉了一次额外的 kernel 调用，也避免了逐元素阶段单独一趟显存往返。

Orthogonal to the occupancy and symmetry optimizations above, execution efficiency remains highly shape dependent. Transformer training repeatedly invokes only a small set of matrix shapes, but the performance-optimal kernel schedule varies considerably across them. Differences in problem size and aspect ratio can substantially change the best tile shape, software pipeline depth, and warp-level schedule, making a single static configuration suboptimal in practice. To accommodate this shape diversity, DMuon treats kernel scheduling as a shape-specific optimization problem(Fig. 6). We implement both the batched and non-batched Gram NS execution paths using tile-level DSLs, including TileLang(29) and CUTE DSL, which allow a large family of schedule variants to be generated from a common kernel template. The autotuner explores a search space spanning tile shapes, software pipeline depths, warp-level scheduling strategies, and memory layouts, and evaluates candidate schedules directly on the target hardware. When a matrix shape is encountered for the first time, DMuon benchmarks the candidate schedules and selects the empirically fastest configuration. The resulting schedule is recorded in a persistent kernel cache keyed by problem shape and execution mode, subsequent invocations of the same shape bypass the search entirely and dispatch the cached configuration directly. This amortization strategy is particularly effective for optimizer workloads, where the same parameter shapes recur throughout training, allowing tuning cost to be paid once while retaining shape-specialized performance for the later steps.

与上述占用率、对称性优化彼此独立的一点是：执行效率仍高度依赖形状。Transformer 训练反复调用的矩阵形状只有少数几种，但这些形状各自最优的 kernel 调度方案差别很大。问题规模与长宽比的不同，会明显改变最佳的 tile 形状、软件流水深度与 warp 级调度，因此在实践中单一固定配置往往是次优的。为适应这种形状多样性，DMuon 把 kernel 调度当作一个按形状取值的优化问题（图 6）。我们用 tile 级 DSL（包括 TileLang (29) 与 CUTE DSL）实现批量化与非批量化两条 Gram NS 执行路径，从而可以从同一个 kernel 模板生成一大类调度变体。自动调优器在 tile 形状、软件流水深度、warp 级调度策略与内存布局构成的搜索空间中探索，并直接在目标硬件上评测候选调度。当某个矩阵形状首次出现时，DMuon 对候选调度做基准测试，选出实测最快的配置；该调度被记入一个持久化的 kernel 缓存，键为问题形状与执行模式。此后同一形状的调用完全跳过搜索，直接派发缓存中的配置。这种摊薄策略对优化器负载尤其有效——相同的参数形状会在整个训练过程中反复出现，因此调优成本只需付一次，而后续步骤仍能享受形状专用的性能。

3.4Computation-Aware Load Balance计算感知的负载均衡

Under our parallel strategy, parameters are distributed across owner ranks at matrix granularity. Because Muon update cost varies substantially with matrix shape, the heterogeneous collection of weight matrices within a model leads to uneven workloads across owners. Without careful assignment, some owners become stragglers while others remain underutilized, causing the optimizer step time to be determined by the slowest rank. A naive round-robin assignment ignores the substantial variation in execution cost across matrix shapes. A more sophisticated approach is Longest-Processing-Time (LPT), which balances workload according to an analytical cost model. However, batching and autotuning introduce a much richer execution space: the runtime of a Muon update depends not only on matrix shape, but also on the selected batch size and kernel schedule. As a result, execution cost can no longer be accurately characterized by a simple analytical proxy.

在我们的并行策略下，参数以矩阵为粒度分布到各个 owner rank 上。由于 Muon 更新的开销随矩阵形状变化很大，模型内形状各异的权重矩阵必然导致各 owner 之间负载不均。若不加仔细指派，一些 owner 会成为拖后腿的掉队者（straggler），另一些则长期闲置，使优化器步骤的耗时由最慢的那个 rank 决定。朴素的轮询（round-robin）分配忽略了不同矩阵形状在执行开销上的巨大差异。更精细的做法是 LPT（Longest-Processing-Time，最长处理时间优先），它依据一个解析代价模型来均衡负载。然而，批量化与自动调优带来了丰富得多的执行空间：一次 Muon 更新的运行时间不仅取决于矩阵形状，还取决于所选的 batch 大小与 kernel 调度。结果是，执行代价已无法再用一个简单的解析代理量准确刻画。

To bound the initialization overhead, we impose a threshold Sthr on the search-space size: when the number of decision variables exceeds Sthr, we fall back to a greedy search assignment instead of solving the MILP exactly. This preserves the near-optimal balance of the MILP solver in the common regime while guaranteeing a bounded, predictable initialization cost at large scale.

但 MILP 的求解时间会随搜索空间规模增长，而搜索空间规模等于不同形状数乘以候选 owner 数。为限定初始化开销，我们对搜索空间规模设一个阈值 Sthr：当决策变量个数超过 Sthr 时，我们退回到贪心搜索分配，而不再精确求解 MILP。这样既在常见规模下保留了 MILP 求解器的近最优均衡效果，又能在超大规模下保证初始化代价有界、可预测。

Forward and backward parameter materialization are issued ahead of use and pipelined with neighboring-layer computation. Gradient reductions are routed to owners while the backward pass continues over earlier layers. Parameter publication is decoupled across steps: after the owner update, updated weights are published asynchronously, and the next step waits only at the pre-forward hook of the layer that consumes the corresponding parameter. 前向与反向的参数物化都提前发起，并与相邻层的计算做成流水。梯度规约在反向过程继续处理更早的层时，就已经被路由到 owner。参数发布则跨步骤解耦：owner 更新之后，更新后的权重被异步发布，下一步只在消费对应参数的那一层的 pre-forward hook 处等待。这种组织方式在保持 Muon 语义完全一致的同时，降低了训练关键路径上实际感受到的优化器开销。

4Implementation实现

DMuon is implemented in approximately 10K lines of Python code with custom kernel sources, packaged as a standalone Python module whose user-facing surface is three calls (dedicate_params, Muon, and the state-dict accessors) and which preserves the standard PyTorch optimizer protocol, and composes with a stock FSDP2 program with no source-level disruption.

DMuon 以约 1 万行 Python 代码（含自定义 kernel 源码）实现，打包为一个独立的 Python 模块：面向用户只有三个入口（dedicate_params、Muon，以及 state-dict 访问器），保持标准 PyTorch 优化器协议，并且能与现成的 FSDP2 程序组合使用，无需在源码层面做任何侵入改动。

Tensor-parallel composition.与张量并行的组合。

Tensor parallelism shards individual weight matrices across a TP group, so a matrix assigned to a single DP owner slot is itself distributed over several ranks. DMuon accommodates this by nesting a second level of ownership within the first: after the cost-aware DP partition (§3.4) assigns each matrix to a DP owner slot, a second pass over the matrices in that slot designates one rank of the TP group as the TP owner responsible for orthogonalizing it. TP-sharded parameters are identified at dedicate_params time by inspecting each parameter’s DTensor placements: any Shard placement on a mesh dimension outside the DP mesh marks the parameter as TP-sharded. We re-read this placement metadata at hook registration rather than caching it, so that future changes to PyTorch’s DTensor internals do not break DMuon. The backward reduction delivers gradients that remain TP-sharded, one slice per rank of the owner’s TP group, these slices are gathered at the TP owner, which assembles the full gradient, runs the same Gram-space Newton–Schulz as in the non-TP case (§3.3), re-partitions the orthogonalized matrix, and scatters each peer its update slice. From this point the step is indistinguishable from the non-TP case: each rank applies its slice locally, and the asynchronous publish broadcasts slices across the DP group as before. TP handling is thus confined entirely to the optimizer step, the forward broadcast, backward reduction, and asynchronous publish all operate on the per-layer slices the host stack already maintains, and neither the TP nor the DP code path is modified. Users compose TP, DMuon, and data-parallel sharding in the same order they would with AdamW.

张量并行把单个权重矩阵切分到整个 TP 组上，因此一个只分配给某个 DP owner 槽位的矩阵，本身又分布在多个 rank 上。DMuon 的做法是在第一层所有权内部再嵌一层：先由代价感知的 DP 划分（§3.4）把每个矩阵分配到某个 DP owner 槽位，然后对该槽位内的矩阵再做一遍分配，指定 TP 组中的某个 rank 作为 TP owner，由它负责正交化。TP 分片的参数在 dedicate_params 时通过检查每个参数的 DTensor placements 来识别：只要在 DP 网格之外的网格维度上出现任何 Shard placement，就把该参数标记为 TP 分片。我们在注册 hook 时重新读取这份 placement 元数据，而不是把它缓存下来，这样日后 PyTorch 的 DTensor 内部实现即使变化，也不会把 DMuon 弄坏。反向规约送来的梯度仍保持 TP 分片形态，owner 的 TP 组上每个 rank 各有一份切片；这些切片在 TP owner 处聚合，由它拼出完整梯度，跑与非 TP 情形相同的 Gram 空间 Newton–Schulz（§3.3），再把正交化后的矩阵重新切分，并把各自的更新切片散发（scatter）给同伴。从这里往后，这一步就与非 TP 情形无法区分了：每个 rank 在本地施加自己的切片，异步发布照旧在 DP 组内广播切片。因此 TP 相关处理完全被限制在优化器步骤之内：前向广播、反向规约与异步发布都作用在宿主栈本来就在维护的按层切片上，TP 与 DP 两条代码路径都没有被改动。用户组合 TP、DMuon 与数据并行分片的顺序，与用 AdamW 时完全一致。

Non-owner placeholder.非 owner 侧的占位符。

On non-owner ranks, the original parameters are replaced with a zero-size placeholder carrying the same dtype. This preserves all module-graph traversal code (Apex, PEFT, gradient clipping libraries that walk model.parameters()) while consuming negligible memory. The owner allocates a full-precision _owned_data tensor on the same device. During unshard, a packed buffer is filled from each owner’s data and exposed back to user code as a persistent nn.Parameter whose storage is the packed buffer, autograd writes the gradient directly to this parameter’s .grad field, avoiding an intermediate copy. Tied parameters (e.g., input embeddings aliased to an output head) are replaced at every alias, so no alias escapes outside DMuon.

在非 owner rank 上，原始参数被替换为一个零尺寸、但 dtype 相同的占位符。这样所有遍历模块图的代码（Apex、PEFT，以及各类会走 model.parameters() 的梯度裁剪库）都能照常工作，而显存占用可以忽略。owner 则在同一个设备上分配一个全精度的 _owned_data 张量。unshard 时，打包缓冲区由各 owner 的数据填充，并作为持久化的 nn.Parameter 暴露回用户代码，其 storage 就是该打包缓冲区；autograd 直接把梯度写入这个参数的 .grad 字段，省掉了一次中间拷贝。绑定参数（例如与输出头共享的输入 embedding）在每一处别名上都会被替换，因此不会有别名逃逸到 DMuon 之外。

Ownership strategy plug-in.所有权策略插件。

The partition layer exposes load_balance, round_robin, and rank0 as named strategies. The latter two exist for ablation: rank0 forces a single fixed owner (all matrices on rank 0) to show what happens if the load-balance discipline is removed entirely. We include them as ablation handles, not as production recommendations.

划分层以命名策略的形式暴露 load_balance、round_robin 与 rank0。后两者是为消融实验而存在的：rank0 强制使用单一固定 owner（所有矩阵都放在 rank 0 上），用来展示完全去掉负载均衡约束会发生什么。我们把它们当作消融开关保留，而不是生产环境的推荐配置。

Configurations.配置。

We adopt the Polar Express (2) coefficient set as the default for k=5 NS steps, with the standard (a,b,c)-quintic coefficients selectable via configuration. The choice is orthogonal to DMuon’s systems contributions. We expose it because the symmetric kernel was tuned for the matrix shapes Polar Express produces in its later steps. A fine-grained analysis of the wgrad distribution over the course of training shows that its dynamic range stays well within what fp16 can represent. We therefore run the NS iteration in fp16 rather than bf16, the two are identical in cost on the tensor cores, but fp16’s three additional mantissa bits give the iteration higher precision at no latency penalty. Across the NS iteration everything stays in fp16 except the on-chip accumulation. The orthogonalized update is cast to fp32 and applied to the fp32 master weights, and then cast back to the parameter’s working dtype (bf16 in our deployments).

对于 k=5 步 NS 迭代，我们把 Polar Express (2) 的系数集作为默认值，标准的 (a,b,c) 五次系数可通过配置选用。这一选择与 DMuon 的系统贡献彼此独立。我们之所以把它开放出来，是因为对称 kernel 是针对 Polar Express 在其后期步骤中产生的矩阵形状调优的。对训练过程中 wgrad 分布所做的细粒度分析显示，其动态范围远在 fp16 可表示的范围内。因此我们用 fp16 而不是 bf16 来跑 NS 迭代：两者在 tensor core 上的开销完全相同，但 fp16 多出的三位尾数让迭代精度更高，而且不带来任何延迟代价。整个 NS 迭代过程中，除片上累加之外一切都保持在 fp16。正交化后的更新被转成 fp32 施加到 fp32 主权重上，然后再转回参数的工作 dtype（在我们的部署中是 bf16）。

The remaining overhead relative to AdamW is not a scheduling artifact, but the irreducible critical-path cost left after load balancing. It is dominated by the Newton–Schulz time of the largest owner-side matrix update, which must still be computed at least once for every Muon step. Thus, DMuon removes the scalable part of Muon’s distributed overhead, but cannot eliminate the final largest NS update without changing the optimizer itself. 5.3Limitations局限性

DMuon is a set of mathematically equivalent reformulations of distributed Muon and does not change the update rule itself. It inherits Muon’s convergence exactly and removes only systems overhead, not algorithmic cost. A limitation of DMuon is that its benefit is less pronounced in single-GPU training. While DMuon still reduces the optimizer-step time by approximately 2×, it cannot leverage the additional benefits provided by distributed execution.

DMuon 是一组对分布式 Muon 在数学上等价的重构，并不改变更新规则本身。它完整继承了 Muon 的收敛性，去掉的只是系统开销，而不是算法成本。DMuon 的一个局限是：在单 GPU 训练中，它的收益不那么明显。此时 DMuon 仍能把优化器单步时间降低约 2×，但无法利用分布式执行带来的额外好处。

6Related Work相关工作

DMuon sits at the intersection of three lines of work: matrix-aware optimizers, their distributed implementations, and hardware-aware kernels for the Newton-Schulz (NS) iteration.

DMuon 处在三条研究脉络的交汇处：矩阵感知优化器、它们的分布式实现，以及面向 Newton–Schulz（NS）迭代的硬件感知 kernel。

Matrix-aware optimizers.矩阵感知优化器。

Shampoo (9) and SOAP (28) precondition updates with Kronecker factors of the empirical Fisher. Muon (11) instead applies an NS polar-factor to the momentum-aggregated gradient, matching their quality with simpler state. Recent variants NorMuon (13), Newton–Muon (8), and Dion (1) adjust or low-rank-ify the orthogonalization. All share the same systems pain point: their per-step update violates the element-wise contract that ZeRO and FSDP assume. DMuon addresses this systems problem. it does not propose a new optimizer.

Shampoo (9) 与 SOAP (28) 用经验 Fisher 矩阵的 Kronecker 因子对更新做预条件。Muon (11) 则对动量累积后的梯度施加 NS 极分解因子，以更简单的状态达到了与它们相当的质量。近期变体 NorMuon (13)、Newton–Muon (8) 与 Dion (1) 对正交化做了调整或低秩化。它们共享同一个系统痛点：其单步更新违反了 ZeRO 与 FSDP 所假设的逐元素契约。DMuon 解决的是这一系统问题，它并不提出新的优化器。

Muon at scale.Muon 的规模化。

Moonlight (15) established that Muon scales (a 16B MoE trained on 5.7T tokens at ∼2× AdamW’s token efficiency), and Kimi-K2 (17) confirmed the result at trillion-parameter scale. Gather-then-compute implementations (§2.3) in which every rank all-reduces the full gradient and runs NS redundantly, a strategy our measurements (Table 1) show carries a significant step-time penalty over AdamW.

Moonlight (15) 确立了 Muon 的可扩展性（一个 16B 的 MoE 在 5.7T token 上训练，token 效率约为 AdamW 的 ∼2×），Kimi-K2 (17) 则在万亿参数规模上确认了这一结果。不过这些工作采用的是「先聚合再计算」的实现（§2.3）——每个 rank 都对完整梯度做 all-reduce 并重复运行 NS；我们的测量（表 1）显示，这种策略相对 AdamW 会带来显著的单步时间代价。

Distributed matrix optimizers.分布式矩阵优化器。

Distributed Shampoo (21) introduced an owner-compute/all-gather paradigm for distributed matrix optimization under data-parallel training: the memory and computation of Shampoo updates are assigned to workers, and the resulting search directions are all-gathered at each step. Canzona (30) addresses a closely related problem in Megatron-style training stacks using an α-balanced data-parallel partition and tensor-parallel micro-group scheduling, reporting a 1.57× end-to-end speedup on Qwen3-32B at 256 GPUs. In contrast, DMuon realizes owner-only Muon as a fine-grained overlap runtime for PyTorch FSDP2/HSDP’s ZeRO-3-style execution, using scheduled broadcasts, two-stage reductions, and asynchronous update publishing to hide communication costs and alleviate peak memory pressure.

Distributed Shampoo (21) 引入了「owner 计算 + all-gather」范式，用于数据并行训练下的分布式矩阵优化：Shampoo 更新的显存与计算被分配到各个 worker 上，得到的搜索方向在每一步被 all-gather。Canzona (30) 在 Megatron 风格的训练栈中处理一个密切相关的问题，采用 α 均衡的数据并行划分与张量并行微组调度，报告在 256 张 GPU 上对 Qwen3-32B 取得 1.57× 的端到端加速。与之不同，DMuon 把「只由 owner 计算」的 Muon 实现为一个细粒度的重叠运行时，面向 PyTorch FSDP2/HSDP 的 ZeRO-3 式执行，通过有调度的广播、两阶段规约与异步更新发布来隐藏通信开销、缓解峰值显存压力。

Sharded training stacks.分片训练栈。

ZeRO (19) partitions optimizer state, gradients, and parameters across DP ranks. FSDP (35) is its PyTorch-native realization, with FSDP2’s DTensor representation adopted in modern stacks (14) and HSDP arranging ranks on a 2-D mesh to bound the all-gather radius. DMuon operates against these sharded-training contracts while preserving their external execution model, requiring no source-level modifications to the host training framework.

ZeRO (19) 把优化器状态、梯度与参数划分到各个 DP rank 上。FSDP (35) 是它在 PyTorch 中的原生实现：现代训练栈 (14) 采用 FSDP2 的 DTensor 表示，HSDP 则把各 rank 安排在二维网格上以限制 all-gather 半径。DMuon 在这些分片训练契约之上运作，同时保持它们对外的执行模型不变，无需对宿主训练框架做任何源码级修改。

Hardware-aware Newton-Schulz.硬件感知的 Newton–Schulz。

Gram Newton-Schulz (34) recasts each NS step as a recurrence in the Gram matrix XX⊤, reducing the work to one symmetric product plus a polynomial in the smaller Gram matrix. Polar Express (2) optimizes per-step coefficients for fixed iteration counts. DMuon adopts both as defaults and contributes a shape-specialized symmetric kernel tuned for the matrix shapes of production LLM and VLA transformers.

Gram Newton–Schulz (34) 把每一步 NS 改写为 Gram 矩阵 XX⊤ 上的递推，把工作量降为一次对称乘积，加上在更小 Gram 矩阵上的一个多项式。Polar Express (2) 针对固定的迭代次数优化每步系数。DMuon 把两者都作为默认采用，并贡献了一个形状专用的对称 kernel，针对生产级 LLM 与 VLA transformer 的矩阵形状调优。

Automatic kernel generation.自动 kernel 生成。

A complementary line of work synthesizes high-performance kernels automatically. Tensor compilers such as TVM (5) search over loop-level schedules, with AutoTVM (6) learning a cost model over hand-written templates, Ansor (36) and FlexTensor (37) generating the search space itself, and Tensor Comprehensions (26) deriving schedules from a polyhedral model. Closer to the hardware, tile-level programming languages Triton (25) and TileLang (29), expose tiles as first-class objects so that near-peak kernels can be written without descending to raw CUDA/PTX. DMuon’s kernel implementation (§3.3) is inspired by these systems.

另一条互补的脉络是自动合成高性能 kernel。TVM (5) 这类张量编译器在循环级调度上做搜索；AutoTVM (6) 在手工模板之上学习代价模型；Ansor (36) 与 FlexTensor (37) 自行生成搜索空间；Tensor Comprehensions (26) 则从多面体模型推导调度。更贴近硬件一侧，tile 级编程语言 Triton (25) 与 TileLang (29) 把 tile 作为一等对象暴露出来，使得无需下降到裸 CUDA/PTX 就能写出接近峰值的 kernel。DMuon 的 kernel 实现（§3.3）正是受这些系统启发。

7Conclusion结论

We presented DMuon, a distributed runtime and kernel stack for scaling the Muon optimizer on modern sharded-training systems. DMuon combines fine-grained communication optimization, computation-aware load balance, and high-performance kernel system to eliminate redundant optimizer computation and overlap the remaining work with training execution. DMuon maintains end-to-end step times within 2% of AdamW on average, making Muon a practical drop-in optimizer for large-scale production training.

我们提出了 DMuon——一个用于在现代分片训练系统上扩展 Muon 优化器的分布式运行时与 kernel 栈。DMuon 把细粒度通信优化、计算感知的负载均衡与高性能 kernel 系统结合起来，消除冗余的优化器计算，并把剩余工作与训练执行重叠。DMuon 让端到端单步时间平均保持在 AdamW 的 2% 以内，使 Muon 成为大规模生产训练中可实际即插即用的优化器。
